## Other people's work inside these scenes

Some scenes contain a piece by someone other than their author: a helper function, or measured data. Each keeps its own terms, and its notice is in the scene's LICENSE file.

- [Abyss](Scenes/abyss/LICENSE): hash functions by David Hoskins (MIT).
- [Caustics](Scenes/caustics/LICENSE): simplex noise by Ashima Arts and Stefan Gustavson (MIT); a hash function by David Hoskins (MIT).
- [Day and Night Sky](Scenes/day-night-sky/LICENSE): a hash function in the form of David Hoskins' (MIT).
- [Eclipse](Scenes/eclipse/LICENSE): hash functions by David Hoskins (MIT).
- [Golden-hour Ocean](Scenes/golden-ocean/LICENSE): the AgX tone-map fit by Benjamin Wrensch (MIT).
- [Heartfelt](Scenes/heartfelt/LICENSE): a hash function its author credits to Dave Hoskins.
- [Liquid Metal](Scenes/liquid-metal/LICENSE): a noise function in the manner of Inigo Quilez's (MIT).
- [Luminescence](Scenes/luminescence/LICENSE): helpers its author credits to Dave Hoskins, Inigo Quilez and Mercury's hg_sdf.
- [Neon Giza](Scenes/neon-giza/LICENSE): helpers by Inigo Quilez (MIT) and Sam Hocevar (WTFPL).
- [Neon Horizon](Scenes/neon-horizon/LICENSE): hash functions by David Hoskins (MIT).
- [Orion Nebula](Scenes/orion-nebula/LICENSE): measured data: VLT/MUSE maps, Weilbacher et al. 2015 (CC0 1.0); 3D dust map, Edenhofer et al. 2024 (CC BY 4.0); star positions from SIMBAD.
- [Rain City](Scenes/rain-city/LICENSE): hash functions by David Hoskins (MIT).
- [Silk](Scenes/silk/LICENSE): a domain-warp layout from an article by Inigo Quilez (MIT).
